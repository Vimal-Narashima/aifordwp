# Fin Bridge Win11 Migration — Self-Service Answers
Date: 2026-08-12

---

## 1. Credentials Vault — Cannot Access

**Try this first:**
1. Sign out of the vault completely, close the browser or app, then sign back in.
2. If you still cannot get in, try from a different browser (e.g. Edge instead of Chrome).

**Still blocked?** Contact the Service Desk and tell them:
- Your name and which team you are in
- How many days the problem has been happening
- The exact error message you see (take a photo of the screen if easier)

---

## 2. Admin Console — Locked Out

**Try this first:**
1. Wait 15 minutes and try signing in again — repeated failed attempts can trigger a temporary lock.
2. Clear your browser cookies and cache, then try again.

**Still locked out?** Contact the Service Desk and tell them:
- Your name and job role
- When the lockout first happened
- Whether anyone else on your team has the same problem

---

## 3. Test VMs — Cannot Connect

**Try this first:**
1. Restart your computer fully (not sleep — a full shutdown and restart), then try connecting again.
2. Check your VPN is connected before attempting to reach the test environment.

**Still cannot connect?** Contact the Service Desk and tell them:
- Your name and which test VM you are trying to reach
- The error message shown when the connection fails
- Whether this started after the Windows 11 update
