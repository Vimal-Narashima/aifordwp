Subject: Some users are experiencing sign-in and lockout issues

We are aware that some users are having trouble signing in and that account lockouts are affecting more people this morning. This is known and the team is actively working on it.

While we investigate, please try this short workaround if you are affected: close all sign-in windows, wait 2 minutes, then try again with your full work email and password. If it still fails, restart your device and try one more sign-in attempt.

Please stay calm and avoid repeated login attempts, as this may make the issue worse for you.

We will share the next update as soon as we have one and no later than later today if there is any change in status.

If you still cannot sign in after the steps above, contact the Service Desk and share the exact error message, the time of your last failed attempt, your username, and whether you restarted your device.
