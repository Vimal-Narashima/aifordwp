## AVD sign-in and account lockouts
1. Close all sign-in windows, wait 2 minutes, then try again with your full work email and password.
2. If it still fails, restart your device and try one more sign-in attempt.

Contact Service Desk if you still cannot sign in after these steps, or if you see “account locked.” Share: exact error message, time of last failed attempt, your username, and whether restart was tried.

## OneDrive missing files and sync errors
1. Open OneDrive from the taskbar cloud icon and check if it says “Paused” or “Signed out”; if yes, sign in and resume sync.
2. In File Explorer, open your OneDrive folder and search for the missing file name to confirm whether it was moved.

Contact Service Desk if files are still missing after these checks. Share: 2-3 missing file names, last time you saw them, any error text in OneDrive, and a screenshot of the cloud icon status.

## Shared drive access denied
1. Disconnect VPN, reconnect, then open File Explorer and try the S: drive again.
2. If it still fails, sign out of Windows and sign back in, then retry S:.

Contact Service Desk if access is still denied after both steps. Share: full error message, the exact folder you were trying to open, time of failure, and whether VPN reconnect and sign-out/sign-in were completed.
