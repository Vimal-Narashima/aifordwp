# All-Staff Notice — Win11 Migration: Known Issues Update
Date: 2026-08-12

---

Hi all,

Following the recent Windows 11 migration, we are aware that some of you are experiencing difficulty with the following:

- Accessing the credentials vault
- Logging in to the admin console
- Connecting to test VMs

We want to reassure you that these issues are known and our team is actively investigating. You do not need to raise a ticket if you are already affected — we have your reports and are working through them as a priority.

**While we investigate, please try the following:**

- **Credentials vault:** Sign out fully, close and reopen your browser, then sign back in. If that fails, try a different browser.
- **Admin console:** Wait 15 minutes before retrying. Clear your browser cookies and cache, then attempt sign-in again.
- **Test VMs:** Do a full restart (not sleep), ensure your VPN is connected, then try again.

We will share a further update by end of business today. If your situation changes or you have a urgent deadline affected, please contact the Service Desk directly.

Thank you for your patience.

— Desktop & Endpoint Team
