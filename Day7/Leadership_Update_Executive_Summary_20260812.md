Week one after migration was mixed for colleagues: many people moved across smoothly and reported better day-to-day performance, but a meaningful group experienced disruption that affected confidence and productivity. The biggest early pain points were sign-in and account access issues, missing files, and access to shared work areas, alongside slower starts for some users and local printing frustrations on one floor. By week two, the overall experience had clearly improved, with sign-in reliability, file availability, and remote connectivity now largely stable for most teams. Sentiment shifted from incident reporting toward normal service, with more users describing the environment as steady and improved compared with the first week.

A small number of issues remain open and are being actively managed. The floor 3 printer problem is still unresolved and is now being treated as an escalation priority because it continues to disrupt routine work. A new issue has also emerged with large spreadsheet files closing unexpectedly for finance users, which is now the top newly raised productivity risk. In parallel, we are investigating a recent policy change linked to slower device startup in the affected business group and are running targeted checks to isolate the exact setting before wider action. We will continue proactive updates, keep interim self-help guidance in place, and provide the next leadership status once the open items have either a verified fix or a clear mitigation path.
