### 1) AVD sign-in and account lockouts (Count: 7)
Why this ranks here:
- This is the highest-volume Blocker theme and prevents users from working at all, so it has the largest immediate productivity and service risk.

One sentence to manager:

### 2) OneDrive missing files and sync errors (Count: 4)
Why this ranks here:
- Although lower volume than some Friction themes, missing files are a Blocker-level data access risk and directly threaten meeting/report delivery.

One sentence to manager:
- "OneDrive missing-file incidents are our second priority because 4 users report potential data unavailability, creating immediate delivery and confidence risk."

### 3) Shared drive access denied (Count: 3)
Why this ranks here:
- Even at lower count, this is still Blocker severity and explicitly blocks critical month-end and finance workflows, so it outranks higher-volume Friction themes.

One sentence to manager:

## Why Higher-Volume Themes Were Not Ranked Above These
- Floor 3 printer mapping failures (6) and cosmetic UI changes (7) have meaningful volume, but they are Friction/Minor rather than complete inability to work.
- Ranking intentionally prioritizes Blocker impact first, then count within those Blockers.
