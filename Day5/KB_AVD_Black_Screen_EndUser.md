## What is happening?

After you sign in to your work desktop, the screen may appear completely black or blank. It might stay that way, or it may sign you out and take you back to the sign-in screen. This is a fault the IT team is already aware of and working to fix.

**Your work and files are not affected.** Nothing you have saved has been lost.

---

## What you can try yourself

**Step 1 — Wait 30 seconds**
Sometimes your desktop comes back on its own. Wait up to 30 seconds without clicking or pressing any keys. If your desktop appears, you can carry on working normally.

**Step 2 — Close the window and sign in again**
If the screen is still black after 30 seconds, close the window completely and sign in again from the beginning. Many people find the second attempt opens properly.

**Step 3 — Try one more time**
If you are signed out again straight away, try once more. If it fails again, stop here — trying repeatedly will not fix the problem itself. Move on to Step 4.

**Step 4 — Use the backup option if IT has sent you one**
If IT has sent you a message with a temporary sign-in link or told you to use a different way to reach your desktop, use that now. If you have not received anything from IT, contact the service desk.

---

## Your documents and emails are safe

Your files are saved on our central systems, not on this screen, so they are completely fine. Documents, emails, and everything else you saved before this started are all still there and will be waiting for you when you sign back in.

---

## Contact the service desk if…

- The black screen appears every time you try to sign in
- You are signed out more than twice in a row
- You cannot get to your desktop at all
- You have an urgent deadline and need your desktop working again

**When you call or log a call or send a request, please have the following ready:**
- Your full name and team or department
- The time you first noticed the problem (for example, "around 8:15 this morning")
- How many times you have tried to sign in
- Whether the screen was completely black or showed anything briefly before going black
- Whether you saw any message in a red or white box on screen, and what it said
- The name of the computer or laptop you are using
