Finalized hypothesis
The most likely cause is the Friday document management app rollout, or a related desktop or shell policy change, affecting shortcut placement on Floor 6. The missing shortcuts may be a deployment side effect, a redirected desktop location, or a profile/shell change rather than deleted files.

Why this is the best-fit hypothesis
- The shortcut issue began after the new document management app was rolled out on Friday afternoon.
- At least one user reported that desktop shortcuts vanished.
- The symptom is consistent with a deployment, profile, or desktop redirection change.
- The evidence does not yet show that this is a floor-wide issue, so that remains to confirm.

Exact remediation steps
1. Identify which shortcuts are missing and confirm whether the issue is limited to one machine or multiple users.
2. Check whether the app is installed successfully on an affected machine.
3. Verify whether the user's Desktop folder is redirected, managed by policy, or relocated to another path.
4. Check whether the shortcuts are missing from the desktop only or also from Start menu, taskbar, or search.
5. If the deployment or policy change removed or relocated the shortcuts, stop or roll back the rollout effect and restore the expected shortcut placement.
6. Recreate any missing shortcuts if the apps are still installed and reachable.

Correct order of operations
1. Confirm the scope and what exactly is missing.
2. Verify app installation and desktop path handling.
3. Check whether the rollout or policy change is responsible.
4. Roll back or correct the deployment impact if confirmed.
5. Restore or recreate shortcuts.
6. Re-test on the affected machine and a control machine.

Verification check after remediation
- Confirm the shortcuts are visible again on the affected user's desktop.
- Confirm the app still launches correctly from the restored shortcuts.
- Confirm the issue does not appear on a second Floor 6 device.

Preventive action to stop recurrence
- Add a pre-deployment check for desktop redirection and shortcut behavior before rolling document management or shell-related changes to the floor.
- Keep a rollback-ready deployment plan for applications that may affect desktop layout or user profile paths.

Operational note
Do not assume the shortcuts were manually deleted until the desktop path and deployment behavior are checked. The timing after the app rollout is the strongest clue.
