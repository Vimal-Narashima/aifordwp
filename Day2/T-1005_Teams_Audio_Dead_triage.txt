TICKET ANALYSIS: T-1005 Teams Audio Dead on Three Machines in the Same Meeting Room
======================================================================================

SUMMARY
-------
Audio functionality non-operational in Microsoft Teams on three devices in the same physical meeting room; other users can hear, but room users cannot.

IMPACT
------
Who:          Three users in same meeting room
How many:     3 devices affected in 1 location
Urgency:      HIGH - Full meeting room unable to participate in audio-based collaboration
Business:     Entire team meeting room offline for audio; meeting effectiveness severely compromised; possible business impact if recurring

KNOWN FACTS
-----------
• Issue affects exactly three devices in same room
• Location: Same physical meeting room
• Audio not working in Teams (calls/meetings)
• [to-verify] All three devices isolated from room audio/video system or independent?
• [to-verify] Other users outside room can hear the three users or not
• [to-verify] Issue is Teams-specific or affects all audio on devices

MISSING INFORMATION TO GATHER
-------------------------------
1. [to-verify] Device types: laptops, desktops, Teams Rooms system, thin clients?
2. [to-verify] Is meeting room equipped with shared audio system (meeting room speakers/mic)?
3. [to-verify] Are the three devices connected to shared meeting room audio hardware?
4. [to-verify] When did audio stop working? (Specific time, recent event, or gradual?)
5. [to-verify] Audio works on one device but not others, or all three completely silent?
6. [to-verify] Microphone and speakers both dead, or one or the other?
7. [to-verify] Do devices show any audio device in Sound Settings, or listed as disabled?
8. [to-verify] Were there recent Windows Updates, driver updates, or room AV changes?
9. [to-verify] Does audio work outside Teams (system sounds, YouTube, Zoom)?

LIKELY CATEGORY
---------------
Hardware/audio driver (shared meeting room system) → Audio settings misconfiguration → Teams client issue → Network/VoIP codec

ROOT CAUSE POSSIBILITIES (to-verify)
- Shared meeting room audio system powered off or disconnected
- Audio driver disabled/corrupted on all three devices (same image or recent update)
- USB meeting room audio device not recognized or driver missing on all three
- Teams audio input/output configured to disabled or null device (group policy applied)
- Meeting room audio system unplugged from network/power or reset
- VoIP codec or audio pathway blocked by firewall/network policy
- Bluetooth audio device connected on all three devices, blocking microphone input
- Meeting room audio system firmware update failed or incompatible with device drivers
- Audio hardware conflicting with VPN client or antivirus software

FIRST DIAGNOSTIC STEP
---------------------
1. Isolate scope (device issue vs room system issue):
   • Step 1a: On one device, check Sound Settings:
     - Settings → System → Sound → [choose device dropdown under Input/Output]
     - Document: Any audio devices listed? Enabled or disabled?
   • Step 1b: Test audio on device OUTSIDE the room:
     - Walk one device out of room, test Teams audio in hallway or different room
     - Document: Does audio work remotely or only fails in this room?

2. Check meeting room audio system:
   • Verify: Meeting room audio system powered on, connected, and active
   • Verify: USB/network cables seated firmly; no indicators of failure
   • Test: Unplug and re-seat main audio device; wait 30 sec, power back on

3. Check Teams device settings:
   • On affected device: Teams → Settings → Devices → Audio devices
   • Document: Which input/output device selected? Is it "Default" or specific hardware?
   • Test: Switch input to different device if available (e.g., built-in mic vs room system)
   • Test: Make short test call; check if audio works with alternate device

4. Check for policy/driver changes:
   • Review: Recent Windows Updates on devices (Settings → Update History)
   • Review: Audio driver status in Device Manager (Audio inputs and outputs)
   • Document: Any devices with yellow warning triangle or unknown device?

5. Widen context:
   • Ask: Have ANY devices in this room successfully used Teams audio today, or all three failed all day?
   • Ask: Does issue affect only Teams or all room audio (Windows system sounds, Zoom, etc.)?

ESCALATION THRESHOLD
--------------------
If room audio system appears offline or damaged → escalate to facilities/AV team
If all devices show audio disabled by policy → check Group Policy and escalate to desktop team
If audio works on device outside room → meeting room audio system issue; escalate to AV team
If remains unresolved → escalate to 2nd line with device audio logs and meeting room system status
