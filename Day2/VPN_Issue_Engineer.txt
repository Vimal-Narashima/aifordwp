Root cause:
Windows 11 upgrade removed legacy VPN client. Intune did not redeploy the new client due to a detection-rule gap.

Action taken:
1. Manually removed stale VPN registry entries under HKLM\SOFTWARE\<vendor>.
2. Force-triggered Intune sync.
3. New VPN client deployed.
4. Split-tunnel config applied.

Verification:
Connectivity confirmed to all internal subnets. No data loss.

Preventive action needed:
Fix/close the Intune detection-rule gap so post-upgrade redeployment of the new VPN client triggers automatically.
